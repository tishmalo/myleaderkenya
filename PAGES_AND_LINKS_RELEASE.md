# Release note — Community Pages & Links on County Pages

## What's new

Citizens can now submit community pages, groups and web links for their county, constituency,
ward, political party or specific aspirant — and the best of them are published on public pages.

A new **county page** is now live for every county (for example `/counties/nyandarua`). Each page
brings together everything voters need for that county in one place:

- The county's approved **aspirants**, with their positions and political parties
- The county's **constituencies** with live aspirant counts per area
- Approved **community pages and links** submitted and reviewed by the public

## How it works for voters

1. A voter signs in and selects **Add Link/Page** from their dashboard menu.
2. They choose a platform (Facebook, WhatsApp, X, Instagram, and more), paste a link, and
   select where it should appear — the whole county, a constituency, a ward, a party, or a
   specific aspirant.
3. Every submission is **reviewed by a moderator before going public**, so the pages stay clean,
   relevant and safe.
4. Once approved, the link appears in the **Pages & Links** section on the matching aspirant
   profiles and on the county's page.

This means a Facebook group like *"Nyandarua County Announcements"* now reaches everyone who
looks up an aspirant or the county — not buried in the group's own feed.

## What's inside

| Area | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| County pages | Aspirants, constituencies with counts, and approved community links per county |
| Aspirant profiles | **Pages & Links** section listing every approved link tagged to that aspirant, their party, or their county |
| Submissions | Account → **Add Link/Page**; contributors can track each submission's status in **My Links** |
| Moderation | A dedicated **Pages & Links** queue in the admin dashboard with approval, rejection and removal |

## For administrators

- New **Pages & Links** entry under *Content* in the admin dashboard.
- Filter the queue by status; approve, reject, or delete in one click.
- Rejected and pending items never appear publicly.

## Stay tuned

County health pages are the foundation. Next we're expanding what each county page can surface —
news, events and verified voter services — so a county page becomes the first stop for every
voter headed to the ballot in 2027.
